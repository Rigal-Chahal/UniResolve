03 / HELP & FAQ

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Quick answers to help you understand the platform and its process.

Go to login ↗
What is UniResolve?＋

UniResolve is a campus grievance portal concept that helps students understand how to raise concerns and follow their progress.

How do I raise a concern?＋

Log in and follow the grievance submission process available in your account. Real submissions require a connected backend.

Can I track my concern?＋

When connected to a database and staff workflow, the portal can show updates such as New, In Progress, and Resolved.

Are these official campus rules?＋

No. The guidelines on this demo are examples. Replace them with verified rules from your institution before launch.

Is my information private?＋

Privacy depends on the production system's security, access controls, and data-handling policy. Do not enter sensitive real information into a demo.
